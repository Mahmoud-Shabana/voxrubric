# Dataset Card — VoxRubric Role and Domain Regression Set

**Dataset ID:** `voxrubric-role-domains`  
**Version:** `1.0.0`  
**License:** Apache-2.0

## Purpose

This synthetic benchmark broadens evaluator regression coverage beyond Python/backend examples. It includes Customer Support, Data Analyst, and Site Reliability Engineering cases.

## Source policy

All questions, answers, rubrics, and failure cases are hand-authored synthetic fixtures.

## Intended use

Use it to confirm that structural metrics such as rubric coverage and follow-up integrity behave consistently across domains and languages.

## Out of scope

These fixtures are not validated hiring assessments and should not be treated as complete role definitions or real applicant data.

## Limitations

The set is small, synthetic, and intentionally focused on evaluator behavior rather than occupational validity or population coverage.
